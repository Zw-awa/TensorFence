# Code of Conduct

[English](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) | [简体中文](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

## Our Pledge

We aim to keep this project respectful, focused, and useful.
Participants are expected to treat others with professionalism and to keep feedback technical.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and direct
- Critique ideas, not people
- Keep discussions on-topic
- Assume good intent until the evidence says otherwise
- Help make issues reproducible and actionable

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Deliberate trolling or disruption
- Public shaming
- Repeatedly ignoring maintainer guidance
- Sharing private information without permission

## Enforcement

Maintainers may close threads, remove content, or restrict participation when behavior harms the project.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any project-related communication.
